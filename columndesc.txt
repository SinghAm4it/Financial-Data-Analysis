Dataset 1: portfolio_data
Description of Columns:

portfolio_id: Unique identifier for each investment portfolio.
portfolio_name: Name of the portfolio (e.g., Retirement Fund, Growth Portfolio).
customer_id: Unique identifier for the customer who owns the portfolio.
total_value: Total value of the portfolio (in USD).
creation_date: Date the portfolio was created.
risk_level: The risk level associated with the portfolio (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

Dataset 2: investment_data
Description of Columns:

investment_id: Unique identifier for each investment.
portfolio_id: Unique identifier for the portfolio (linked to portfolio_data).
asset_type: Type of the asset (e.g., Stock, Bond, Mutual Fund, Real Estate).
asset_name: Name of the asset or security (e.g., Apple, Tesla, S&P 500 ETF).
units: Number of units of the asset held in the portfolio.
purchase_price: Price at which the asset was purchased (per unit in USD).
current_price: Current market price of the asset (per unit in USD).
purchase_date: Date when the asset was purchased.
market_value: Current market value of the asset (units * current_price).

Dataset 3: transaction_data
Description of Columns:

transaction_id: Unique identifier for each transaction.
portfolio_id: Unique identifier for the portfolio (linked to portfolio_data).
transaction_type: Type of transaction (e.g., Buy, Sell, Dividend).
transaction_date: Date the transaction occurred.
asset_type: Type of the asset (linked to investment_data).
asset_name: Name of the asset (linked to investment_data).
units: Number of units involved in the transaction.
transaction_price: Price per unit of the asset at the time of the transaction.
total_amount: Total amount involved in the transaction (units * transaction_price).

Dataset 4: performance_data
Description of Columns:

portfolio_id: Unique identifier for the portfolio (linked to portfolio_data).
date: Date of the performance snapshot.
portfolio_value: Total value of the portfolio on the given date (in USD).
return_percentage: Portfolio return percentage on the given date.
benchmark_return: Return percentage of the relevant market benchmark (e.g., S&P 500) on the same date.
net_inflows: Net inflows into the portfolio (total deposits - withdrawals) on the given date (in USD).
net_outflows: Net outflows from the portfolio (withdrawals - deposits) on the given date (in USD).

Dataset 5: customer_data
Description of Columns:

customer_id: Unique identifier for each customer.
customer_name: Name of the customer.
age: Age of the customer.
gender: Gender of the customer (Male, Female, Other).
income: Annual income of the customer (in thousands of USD).
location: City or region where the customer resides.
signup_date: Date the customer signed up for the portfolio management service.

Explanation of the Datasets:
Portfolio Data: Contains 200 investment portfolios with their total value, creation date, and risk level.
Investment Data: Tracks 1,000 individual investments within the portfolios, including asset types, units held, and their current market value.
Transaction Data: Contains 3,000 transactions (buy, sell, or dividends) for assets within the portfolios.
Performance Data: Tracks portfolio performance over time, including returns, portfolio value, net inflows/outflows, and comparison to benchmark returns.
Customer Data: Includes demographic data for 500 customers who own investment portfolios.
